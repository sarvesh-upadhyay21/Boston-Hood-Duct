Professional Kitchen Hood Cleaning, Grease Duct Scraping & Rooftop Exhaust Fan Degreasing

At Boston Hood & Duct, we specialize in commercial kitchen exhaust hood cleaning and inspections, grease duct scraping, rooftop exhaust fan degreasing, and fire code compliance support for restaurants, hotels, healthcare facilities, schools, universities, and institutional kitchens throughout Greater Boston and Massachusetts. Our services focus on removing combustible grease, inspecting accessible exhaust system components, documenting findings, and supporting applicable NFPA 96, Massachusetts 527 CMR 1.00 (Chapter 50), and Boston Fire Department (BFD) requirements. Cleaner Exhaust Systems. Reduced Fire Hazards. Greater Peace of Mind.

Scope of Services:

1. Type I Hood Canopy & Plenum Cleaning: Professional degreasing of accessible commercial kitchen hood interiors, plenums, and grease collection surfaces using appropriate cleaning methods.
2. Grease Duct Cleaning & Bare-Metal Scraping: Mechanical scraping and removal of accumulated grease from accessible horizontal and vertical exhaust ducts, using suitable cleaning methods to achieve required cleanliness.
3. Rooftop Exhaust Fan Degreasing: Cleaning of accessible rooftop exhaust fan blades, housings, grease collection components, and associated exhaust surfaces.
4. Grease Filter & Baffle Cleaning: Degreasing of stainless steel grease filters, baffles, and grease collection devices to remove accumulated cooking residues.
5. Commercial Kitchen Exhaust System Inspections: Inspection of accessible exhaust components for grease accumulation, cleaning deficiencies, and visible conditions requiring maintenance or corrective action.
6. NFPA 96 & Massachusetts Fire Code Support: Cleaning and inspection services designed to support applicable NFPA 96, Massachusetts 527 CMR 1.00, and local fire prevention requirements.
7. Inspection & Cleaning Service Labels: Application of required service labels documenting inspection and cleaning dates, provider information, and applicable compliance or noncompliance findings.

NFPA 96 & Massachusetts 527 CMR Kitchen Exhaust Compliance Schedule

How Often Must Restaurant Hoods Be Inspected in Massachusetts? Commercial kitchen exhaust inspection frequency depends on cooking operations and grease accumulation.

Cooking OperationInspection FrequencyRegulatory Context
Solid-fuel cooking — wood, charcoal, mesquite
MonthlyHighest fire risk requiring frequent grease depth measurement and inspection.
High-volume cooking — 24-hour kitchens, charbroiling, wok cooking
Every 3 monthsContinuous grease vapors demand quarterly certified inspection and cleaning.
Moderate-volume commercial cooking — typical restaurant kitchens
Every 6 monthsStandard casual full-service dining restaurants and school kitchens.
Low-volume or seasonal cooking — certain institutional kitchens
AnnuallySeasonal facilities, churches, day-use senior centers, and light snack kitchens.

Important Notice: Cleaning obligations depend on applicable state and local requirements and system conditions. Boston restaurants must also follow Boston Fire Department cleaning and documentation requirements.
