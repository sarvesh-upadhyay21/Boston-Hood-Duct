Terms of Service

BOSTON HOOD & DUCT commercial service terms, performance standards, and operational guidelines across Massachusetts.

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Agreement to Terms

By accessing our website or engaging BOSTON HOOD & DUCT LLC for commercial kitchen exhaust system cleaning, you agree to these Terms of Service and applicable NFPA 96, Boston Fire Department, and Massachusetts fire safety regulations.

2. Scope of Service & Standards

All cleaning services are performed in accordance with NFPA Standard 96 (Standard for Ventilation Control and Fire Protection of Commercial Cooking Operations). Work involves degreasing accessible exhaust components to bare metal. Inaccessible duct runs without code-compliant access panels will be documented in writing.

3. Site Access & Utilities

Clients are responsible for providing unobstructed access to kitchen cooklines, electrical panels, rooftop terminations, and operational water and power supplies during agreed service windows.

4. Certification & Documentation

Upon successful service completion and receipt of invoice payment, BOSTON HOOD & DUCT affixes an official Certificate of Performance to the hood canopy and provides digital before/after photographic documentation.

5. Inquiries & Dispatch

Direct all contractual inquiries to: service@bostonhoodandduct.com or call +1 (857) 800-0890.
